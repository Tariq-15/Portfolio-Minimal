Sales CRM System

Building a single source of truth for 15,000+ student records inside Google Sheets, without a database, and without slowing anyone down.

I joined ACS Future School as Product Manager, Tech at a point where enrolment data lived in whichever spreadsheet, form response, or inbox someone had last touched it in. There was no single place to ask "how many students enrolled this month," let alone get an answer that everyone trusted.

This is the story of how that became one CRM, built entirely on Google Sheets and Apps Script, now holding more than 15,000 records.

The Problem

Student enrolment data was dispersed across manual records, form exports here, a WhatsApp-coordinated spreadsheet there, and no unified tracking or reporting pipeline connecting any of it. Nobody could see enrolment trends as they happened; by the time a report was assembled by hand, the numbers were already a week stale.

There was no engineering team to hand this to, and no budget for a dedicated database. The constraint wasn't really technical, it was that the tool had to be something the existing team could actually operate day to day, without a developer on call.

The Approach

I designed the ingestion pipeline around what the team already used, Google Forms and Sheets, and layered automation underneath it rather than replacing it. Apps Script pulls API-extracted enrolment data on a schedule, a de-duplication layer catches repeat entries before they pollute the dataset, and live reporting dashboards sit on top, reading from the same sheet everyone else already trusts.

Enrolment Forms → Apps Script Ingestion → De-duplication → Live Dashboard
The pipeline, end to end, no database, no engineering team.

The hardest part wasn't the automation itself, Apps Script is forgiving enough for that. It was resisting the urge to over-engineer a solution nobody but me would be able to maintain. Every decision got measured against one question: could the next person who touches this understand it in an afternoon?

The Outcome

  • 15,000+ student records consolidated into a single, continuously current source of truth
  • Zero manual data entry once the pipeline went live1
  • Real-time enrolment reporting, replacing a process that previously took days to assemble

Manual data entry has been eliminated, enrolment tracking is available in real time, and, maybe more importantly, the system keeps running without me having to think about it. That was always the actual goal.

What I'd Do Differently

If I started this today, I'd introduce version history on the automation scripts much earlier, the first few months of changes lived only in my memory, which is a terrible place to store production logic. Everything since has a proper changelog.

I'd also push back sooner on the instinct to keep adding features. The CRM's biggest strength turned out to be doing a small number of things reliably, not doing everything.

1 Aside from the occasional record that arrives outside the expected form fields, those still get a human's eyes before they're merged in.

Thanks to the ACS Future School team for trusting an all-Sheets system to do a database's job.
