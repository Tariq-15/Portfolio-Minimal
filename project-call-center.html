Case Study · 2024, Present

Call Centre & CLM Journey

Giving customer issues a defined path, from first contact to resolution and follow-up, instead of whoever happened to pick up the case.

At ACS Future School, incoming customer issues had no defined path. There was no consistent owner for a case, no escalation route once it stalled, and no shared way to say with confidence how something had actually been resolved.

This is the story of how that became a call centre solution paired with a complete Customer Lifecycle Management journey, now handling more than 400 issues a month.

The Problem

Customer issues were handled without structure. No defined Customer Lifecycle Management (CLM) journey existed, so support requests were addressed inconsistently, with neither clear ownership nor an escalation path. Two agents could handle the same kind of issue two different ways, and there was no shared record of which approach had actually worked.

The Approach

I built a call centre solution capable of handling incoming issues at scale, and paired it with a complete CLM journey defining exactly how a customer progresses, from first contact, through resolution, to follow-up. Every stage got an explicit owner and a defined next step, rather than leaving escalation to whoever happened to be free.

The CLM journey wasn't a separate document nobody read, it was built into how a case actually moved through the system, so following the process was the easiest way to close a case, not an extra step on top of it.

The Outcome

  • 400+ issues handled per month with a consistent process underpinning every interaction
  • Clear ownership, every case has a defined next step and an owner responsible for it
  • One shared framework, the CLM journey gives the team a common way to manage the whole customer relationship, not just the individual ticket

What I'd Do Differently

If I started this again, I'd instrument the handoffs between CLM stages from day one rather than relying on the team's own diligence to follow the journey. A lightweight status check that flags a case stalled between stages would catch the edge cases sooner than waiting for a customer to chase it themselves.

Delivered for ACS Future School's support operation, now running on a consistent, shared customer lifecycle process.
